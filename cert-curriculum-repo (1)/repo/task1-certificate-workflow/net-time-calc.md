| Input | Value | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Requests per semester | 400 | ASSUMPTION |
| Staff min/request, current | 14 | ASSUMPTION |
| Staff min/request, proposed | 6 | ASSUMPTION |
| Weekly exception review | 20 min x 16 wk | ASSUMPTION |
| Spot-check audit | 10% of requests x 3 min | ASSUMPTION |

Gross saving = 400 x (14-6) = **3200 min**

Overhead = 320 + 120 + 200 + 180 = **820 min**

**Net saved = 2380 min = 39.7 h per semester**

Steady-state (setup already paid): 2560 min = 42.7 h per semester.

## Sensitivity (net hours per semester)

| Requests/sem | saving 4 min | saving 6 min | saving 8 min |
|---|---|---|---|
| 200 | 2.3 | 9.0 | 15.7 |
| 400 | 13.0 | 26.3 | 39.7 |
| 800 | 34.3 | 61.0 | 87.7 |

All cells stay positive here, but the gain shrinks fast: at 200 requests and 4 min saving it is only about 2 h per semester, barely above the review effort. Below roughly 155 requests per semester at that saving, the change would cost more than it saves.
