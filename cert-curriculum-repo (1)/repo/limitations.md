# Limitations
- All data is synthetic or assumed; nothing was observed at a real college.
- The 20-case baseline is too small for firm rates (25% rework has roughly an 11%-47% interval).
- Tests check the prototype's own rules; they do not show real-world adoption or correctness.
- Task 2 covers semesters I-VII only (152 credits); semester VIII and programme/branch/version are missing, and the official syllabus and regulations were not available, so the course list rests on the student portal alone and regulatory fit is unchecked.
- Three job postings (one aggregator, one senior role) are a very small sample; the five AI exercises are small and were run during drafting.
- No faculty or graduate feedback.
- No security review; the prototype is not a production system.
